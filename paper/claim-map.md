## Dataset roles

| Dataset | Diagnostic role |
| --- | --- |
| Hospital | Damaged names connect word forms, retained candidates and entity knowledge; syllable names also expose a construction regularity. |
| Movies | Source-specific mappings contrast with candidate-preserving errors; assigned-director conflicts test preservation separately. |
| Beers | Reversible formatting gives a model-dependent familiarity boundary and location-conflict control. |
| Rayyan | Spreadsheet transformations expose the reference's role in repair credit through independent field checks and actual-input scoring. |

Recoverability is a cell-level property, not a label for an entire dataset. Rayyan is not a fourth four-condition experiment; Flights and Tax serve only the continuation probe. [Limitations](../docs/limitations.md) and [six companions](companions/README.md) retain the full scope and criteria.
