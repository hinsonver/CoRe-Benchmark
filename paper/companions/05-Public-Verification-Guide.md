# CoRe executable verification guide

This candidate includes the manuscript after VE-M01–M05. Start with [reproduction and scoring](../../docs/reproduction.md), [figure/table navigation](../../docs/results.md), [the complete compiled-label map](../result-map.json) and [data acquisition](../../docs/data.md). The [revision binding](../revision-binding.json) identifies its exact paper and synchronized presentation sources.

| Question | Public entry | Historical or scoring entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1: what evidence supports recovery? | `python tools/core.py reproduce --output build/replayed` recomputes condition contrasts, intervals, significance, seed/scale products and current displays | The external historical route re-scores archived Movies P2 and aligned replies; P1 remains a saved-summary projection |
| C2: are assigned values preserved? | The same replay recomputes directed rewrite rates on archived known-entity indicators | Exact historical validation re-scores the nine-model Movies conflict evidence with its bound screening files |
| C3: what does repair credit reward? | `python tools/check_rayyan.py` replays the field-audit estimate and re-scores actual TabClean input/output | The scoring check preserves version differences and excludes 150 development rows; saved title matches and field-check criteria remain the audit basis |

Current replay produces 19 table sources and nine figure CSVs. The main paper contains seven figures and eighteen tables, including the full Hospital example, aligned/whole-table matrix and eight-model scale plot. The overview connects recovery evidence, assigned values and repair credit as diagnostic questions; the three checks retain their own inputs. The Rayyan plot and complete acceptance table remain extended displays. `--presentation frozen` selects the original experimental formatting.

Public Movies replay uses numerical witnesses, not row-level movie records. Exact archived-output validation uses the separate local historical archive and a truthful external local-use record; neither is automatically a public release. P1 uses saved summaries and the historical route does not independently re-score every E4/E5 output. Rayyan includes selected scalar rechecks, not a new PubMed retrieval. See [scope](../../docs/limitations.md) for these distinctions and [data acquisition](../../docs/data.md) for the installer.

Authors are recorded in [CITATION.cff](../../CITATION.cff). This is a local submission candidate, not a published repository or a formal reproducibility-evaluation approval.
