This candidate includes the manuscript after VE-M01–M05. Start with [reproduction and scoring](../../docs/reproduction.md), [figure/table navigation](../../docs/results.md), [the complete compiled-label map](../result-map.json) and [data acquisition](../../docs/data.md). | Question | Public entry | Historical or scoring entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1: what evidence supports recovery? | `python tools/core.py reproduce --output build/replayed` recomputes condition contrasts, intervals, significance, seed/scale products and current displays | The external historical route re-scores archived Movies P2 and aligned replies; P1 remains a saved-summary projection |

Current replay produces 19 table sources and nine figure CSVs. The main paper contains seven figures and eighteen tables, including the full Hospital example, aligned/whole-table matrix and eight-model scale plot. The overview connects recovery evidence, assigned values and repair credit as diagnostic questions; the three checks retain their own inputs. The Rayyan plot and complete acceptance table remain extended displays. `--presentation frozen` selects the original experimental formatting.

Public replay uses numerical witnesses for Movies and 138 externalized relay/TabClean originals. [A04 scope](../../docs/release-scope.md) adds paired counts, six anonymous conflict groups and actual-input Rayyan outcomes. Original-evidence validation requires all 427 external dependency/scorer members and a truthful local-use record; it does not approve sharing. P1 uses saved summaries; 150 early Hospital blocks lack row mappings and remain saved-count projections; E4/E5 are not comprehensively rescored. Rayyan includes scalar rechecks, not new retrieval. See [limitations](../../docs/limitations.md) and [data instructions](../../docs/data.md).

Authors are recorded in [CITATION.cff](../../CITATION.cff). This is a local submission candidate, not a published repository or a formal reproducibility-evaluation approval.
