# Scope and limitations

CoRe is a controlled evaluation protocol, not a new cleaning algorithm. Its frozen results concern eight main-comparison LLMs, three controlled data families and a separate Rayyan reference audit, on English tables concerning mainly US entities. Hospital's damaged names, Movies' source-specific mappings and retained candidates, Beers' information-preserving formatting errors, and Rayyan's reference transformations provide complementary diagnoses. The selection supports mechanism-specific interpretation, not a prevalence estimate across cleaning workloads. Transfer to private data also depends on size, sparsity, task complexity and supporting records. The [claim map](../paper/claim-map.md) states the supported conclusions.

Original, word and syllable conditions pair rows and error positions; fresh records can differ in difficulty. Hospital F largely revisits the same institutions in a newer source. Beers F differs in brewery concentration and alcohol-content distributions, so it is descriptive. Name substitutions also change surface form. Hospital's restricted syllable inventory is partly recoverable by GPT-6.1-Sol. The tables retain one accepted error-strength exception, documented in the [construction companion](../paper/companions/01-构造与验收细则.md).

The continuation probe found no positive evidence of benchmark-row memorization in the tested models and samples. This is not proof of absent memorization or training contamination. Original-minus-fresh differences are not a pure memorization estimate. Movies knowledge claims rely on the commercial models because open-model repair scores are near zero. The scale trend concerns Qwen2.5's total Hospital effect. Beers is a model-dependent boundary case. Knowledge conflicts mainly affect attributes with distinctive real-world values, such as film directors; they do not measure how often such conflicts occur in private data.

Intervals summarize fixed constructions and archived replies, not repeated API calls. Chunk resampling does not separately estimate dependence between chunks sharing an entity. Entity resampling in conflict tests leaves shared-chunk dependence outside its uncertainty estimate. Whole-table methods receive supporting records unavailable in the main chunk setting.

The default Movies route replays numerical witnesses rather than re-judging row-level replies. The optional exact historical route re-scores archived cleaning, aligned and conflict outputs. The continuation probe remains a saved-summary projection, and E4/E5 are not all independently re-scored. Exact historical validation and data-access requirements are described in the [data guide](data.md).

Rayyan replay uses saved title matches, retained field-check outcomes and saved encoding outcomes. It includes 114 scalar rechecks but does not reconstruct the full PubMed candidate responses or all encoding checks. The 95.2% estimate (95% interval 91.2–97.8%) is a weighted reference-check failure rate among title-matched sampled cells under field-specific criteria, using the 948-cell type composition. It is not the fraction of erroneous literature records. Its interval conditions on the saved matches and checks and excludes matching uncertainty and PMID-level dependence. Date checks test agreement with publication year despite undocumented exact field semantics; volume/issue checks test presence. See the [Rayyan companion](../paper/companions/03-Rayyan检索与评分材料说明.md).

Reproducing the archived statistics does not make a fresh model run identical or establish external correctness for every reference cell. The study scope and evidence are frozen; this assembly adds navigation and execution support without new models, datasets, seeds, baselines or scientific results.
