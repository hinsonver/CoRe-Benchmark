# Beers provenance

The original table is inherited from the Raha/Baran Beers benchmark, whose
provenance note identifies CraftCans.com. The fresh table was queried from
Wikidata on 2026-09-29; its source records are CC0. Synthetic numeric identifiers
and injected errors were added by the experiment code. Surrogate tables replace
beer, brewery, and city identity terms while retaining the controlled errors.

Wikidata-derived fresh records are marked CC0, but redistribution of the
inherited benchmark table remains to be reviewed separately.
